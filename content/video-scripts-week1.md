## Boundaries checklist (recheck before publishing any video)

- No romantic or suggestive wording, framing, music or imagery. Use "friendly," "company,"
  "conversation," "calm," "comfort" — never "date," "intimate," or similar.
- Cuddling and companionship content: clients stay clothed/covered, consent-based framing,
  companionship is explicitly public-place only.
- No content implies anything sexual is on offer, directly or by innuendo.
- Every video ends with a clear, honest CTA to WhatsApp — no fake urgency or fake scarcity.
